# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Avinash's personal portfolio includes work, writing, interests, and small interactive experiences. Games give visitors short, replayable challenges within that same site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first games are pattern memory and number memory, played locally without accounts.
- Personal bests are stored on the current device. Sound is optional.
- Games share a reusable architecture and visual language, with responsive, mobile-friendly controls.
- Accounts, friend challenges, and live multiplayer are future possibilities, not current capabilities.
- Existing implementation: Astro, TypeScript, Geist fonts, shared page layout, light/dark themes.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the portfolio's existing identity. Games should be minimal and playful, with small moments of delight rather than a separate arcade website.

## Product Principles

- Make the game easy to understand and quick to replay.
- Share session behavior and presentation across games; keep rules independent.
- Preserve the rest of the portfolio when adding new experiences.
- Never require sound or a mouse to understand the game.
